Introduction to Computer Technology

Computer technology is the study and use of computers, digital systems, software, networks, and information technologies. It is used in education, business, communication, science, entertainment, and many other areas of modern life.

A computer system generally consists of hardware and software. Hardware includes physical components such as the processor, memory, storage, keyboard, mouse, and display. Software provides the instructions that allow the hardware to perform useful tasks.

Learning Objective

After completing this lesson, students should be able to explain the basic components of a computer system and describe the difference between hardware and software.
